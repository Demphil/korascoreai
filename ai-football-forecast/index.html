Statistics meet match context

Recent results can reveal form, but rarely tell the whole story. Evidence gathering considers team performance and previous meetings, adding news, absences, weather and official squad information when available. One report helps you connect these factors instead of switching between separate pages.

Nexus Engine and Oracle Core

The analysis pipeline has complementary roles: gathering evidence and reading tactical factors, then reviewing the scenario and conclusion. The interface follows actual processing progress. A waiting state stays visible; an animated screen does not mean that a forecast is complete or that a team is certain to win.

A report you can revisit

Available reports include an estimated score and home-win, draw and away-win probabilities, with a summary and factors behind the assessment. Choose a supported app language to read the report. Forecast Vault keeps your requests and reports together, so you can leave the processing screen and return to the request later.

Start with a match you care about

Install from Google Play if you are an eligible tester, sign in with Google and choose an available match in Forecast. Check the displayed Coins cost before confirming a request. Not every fixture qualifies for analysis: the system may withhold a forecast when evidence is insufficient, as shown in the request status.

Forecast questions

Can I generate a forecast on the website?

The website explains the feature and previews its presentation. Requesting and reading Forecast reports are available inside the Android app.

Is the predicted score guaranteed?

No. It is an estimate based on available information. Team performance can change and important data may arrive late. Read the probabilities and supporting factors alongside the score.

Can everyone download the app now?

The app is currently in closed testing. Your Google account must be eligible for the test; a store link does not automatically grant eligibility.

Forecasts are sports estimates. Coverage and available evidence vary by match.
