What do Coins unlock?

Coins unlock KoraScoreAi content at the displayed price. A Forecast request and an Insights analysis may have different costs; check the screen before confirming. Coins are not a wager on a match, and they are not awarded for a team win or a correct prediction.

Choose a PayPal Coins pack

The wallet offers packs of 100, 500 and 1500 Coins. Review the displayed USD price before continuing to PayPal, where the order details are shown. Coins are credited after the payment is confirmed and verified, not simply when an order is created or PayPal opens.

Surveys: a reward after confirmation

Eligible surveys can earn Coins when offers match your country and profile. Credit follows confirmation by the provider. Opening a survey page or receiving a no-surveys message does not earn Coins. PayPal purchases provide another option when survey availability is limited.

Review your balance and orders

Sign in with Google to connect the wallet to your account. Review your Coins balance and PayPal order status in the wallet. A pending or incomplete payment does not mean credit has arrived; refreshing the order status does not make a new payment.

Forecast questions

Do Forecast and Insights share a wallet?

Yes. Coins credited to your account can be used for both features.

Can Coins be withdrawn as money?

No. Coins unlock content, have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn or converted into money.

Does opening a survey earn Coins?

No. An eligible reward must be confirmed by the provider, and survey availability depends on country and profile.
