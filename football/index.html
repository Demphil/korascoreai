Find today's football fixtures

All Games groups football fixtures and results by date. Use the date strip to move between yesterday, today and tomorrow, then choose all matches, live games, upcoming fixtures or finished results. Kickoff times use Morocco time; check the displayed date and time when following a match from another timezone.

Follow live football scores

Live shows the current score and match clock where coverage is available. Sort by competition or kickoff time and open a match to explore its available details. Scores refresh periodically and some competitions can have delays. The last-updated time helps you judge how recent the board is.

Explore leagues and national teams

Browse competitions by country, including international football and African and Asian competitions where coverage is available. Search for a competition to find its fixtures, results and available standings without browsing every match on the main board.

Save your favourites

Match browsing is available without an account. Sign in with Google to save favourite teams and matches to your account and follow them across devices. Insights and forecasts are separate account features. Football forecasts are estimates, not guaranteed match outcomes.

Frequently asked questions

Can I browse scores without signing in?

Yes. All Games and Live are available without creating an account. Account features require Google sign-in.

Does every competition update instantly?

Coverage varies by competition. Scores may be delayed and some match details may be unavailable. Check the match state and last-updated time.

How do I find tomorrow's fixtures or yesterday's results?

Open All Games, choose the date on the date strip, then select upcoming fixtures or finished results.
