Request account deletion

You can request deletion of your KoraScoreAi account and associated data without reinstalling the app. Email feesrock@gmail.com from your registered address with the subject “Delete KoraScoreAi account” and state that you want your account and data deleted. Do not send passwords or bank details. In the app: Profile → Request account deletion. We review the request and verify ownership, and explain any data retained for legal obligations, purchase disputes or security and its applicable retention period. A request is not immediate deletion; uninstalling does not delete your account.
